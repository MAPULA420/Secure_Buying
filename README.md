Secure_Buying
Sellers and customers on TikTok and Facebook meet scams daily. A customer can pay and never get the good. A customer can claim they didn't receive a product they did get, ruining the seller's reputation. A seller can send the wrong good. And a customer can send fake proof of payment.

Secure_Buying protects both sides by holding the payment in the app until the deal is done.

How It Works
Seller and customer register with their ID.
The seller photographs the exact item and the buyer confirms it's the right product.
The customer pays the app, not the seller. No payment means no order is sent.
If the product isn't delivered and confirmed within 7 days of payment, the customer is refunded automatically.
When the product arrives, the buyer scans their ID to confirm receipt. This starts a one-day window to check for damage.
If nothing is reported in that day, the seller gets paid.
The seller pays the delivery fee upfront and gets it back when they're paid.
If the product arrives damaged, the customer gets a full refund once the seller confirms the product is returned. "Wrong product" claims don't apply, because the buyer approved the photo before shipping.
A change of mind is not covered. That's the seller's business.
Built With
Java and AWS (S3 for photos, DynamoDB for users and transactions). This is a proof of concept, and payment is simulated.

Where It Started
This project began as a Solidity smart contract, which is in the contracts/ folder. I then rebuilt it on AWS for the Cloud Computing elective.
